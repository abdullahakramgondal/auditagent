Write a realistic supply agreement between Karakoram Software Pvt Ltd (the "Buyer") and Gulberg Computers (the "Vendor") for IT equipment, as it would appear in the procurement files of a Pakistani software company.

Length and style
- About 3,500 to 4,500 words, in formal legal English.
- Numbered clauses ("1.", "1.1", "2." and so on) with short headings. Include parties, definitions, term, scope of supply, delivery, price, payment, warranty, confidentiality, liability, termination, force majeure, governing law (Pakistan), notices and a signature block.
- Write every commercial term below in YOUR OWN natural legal wording, in the clause where it belongs. Do not copy my sentences. Write some numbers as words and digits together (for example "thirty (30) days") and others as digits only.

Commercial terms that must each appear exactly once, with exactly these values:
- Late delivery penalty: for each day of delay after 28 February 2027, a penalty of 2.5% of the invoice value applies, up to a maximum total of 15%. The first 4 days of delay carry no penalty.
- Volume discount: an order of 30 or more units in a single order receives a 7% discount.
- Agreed unit price: Dell UltraSharp U2723QE Monitor at PKR 85,000 per unit; the invoice must not exceed it.
- Payment terms: each invoice must be paid within 28 days of the invoice date.

How to write these terms
- Late delivery penalty: do not write the delivery date inside the penalty clause. Write it once, in the delivery clause, as a defined term of your own choice (a capitalised term in quotation marks and brackets), and in the penalty clause refer to the date only by that term.

Everything else
- Do NOT add any other penalty, discount, rebate, price, price list, fee, interest rate or payment deadline beyond the terms above and the decoy clauses below.
- Include these decoy clauses. They are ordinary boilerplate and must stay exactly as described:
- A limitation of liability clause capping each party's liability at 10% of the total order value.
- A late payment charge of 2% per month that the Vendor may charge if the BUYER pays late.
- Termination by either party on thirty (30) days written notice.
- A warranty of twelve (12) months from delivery.
- The Buyer shall inspect the Equipment within seven (7) days of delivery; Equipment not rejected in writing within that period is deemed accepted.
- A restocking fee of 5% of the price of the returned goods applies to undamaged Equipment that the Buyer asks to return after acceptance.

Output only the contract text. No commentary, no markdown tables, no placeholders such as [insert].
