Write the text of a purchase order from Gulberg Computers (the "Vendor") to Karakoram Software Pvt Ltd (the "Buyer"), for IT equipment, as a Pakistani IT supplier would print it.

Format
- Plain text only: no markdown, no bold, no tables drawn with | or - characters.
- One page, about 15 to 30 lines. One line per item row: the description and the numbers on the same line, separated by at least two spaces, unless the notes say otherwise.
- Label every value with a natural label of your own choice; do not copy my wording. Dates may be written in any natural format unless the notes say otherwise.

Values that must each appear exactly once, exactly as given:
- Order number: PO-2026-0903 (write it exactly as given)
- Order date: 20 October 2026
- Required delivery date: 25 November 2026
- Item (write the name exactly as given): Samsung 32-inch 4K UHD Smart Monitor with Height-Adjustable Stand; quantity 12; unit price PKR 58,000; amount PKR 696,000
- Total: PKR 696,000

Notes on how to print it
- Print the item description over two lines: the first part of the description and the numbers on the first line, the rest of the description on the line below. Together the two lines must read as the exact item name given.

Everything else
- Do NOT add any other amount, tax, discount, fee, deposit, date or reference number. No phone numbers, NTN or bank account numbers. A footer line is allowed but must not contain numbers.

Output only the document text. No commentary, no placeholders such as [insert].
